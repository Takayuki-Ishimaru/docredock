The DocRedock desktop GUI uses the following runtime and build packages:

- Avalonia UI and its Desktop, Fluent theme, platform, rendering, remote
  protocol, and build packages — MIT
- SkiaSharp and platform-native asset packages — MIT
- HarfBuzzSharp and platform-native asset packages — MIT
- MicroCom.Runtime — MIT
- Tmds.DBus.Protocol — MIT
- Avalonia.Angle.Windows.Natives — BSD-3-Clause

Exact versions and the complete resolved GUI dependency graph are pinned in
`src/DocRedock.Gui/packages.lock.json`.

The test project uses the following development-only packages. Their licenses
must remain on the enterprise permissive allowlist and must be revalidated when
the locked dependency graph changes:

- xUnit.net — Apache-2.0
- xunit.runner.visualstudio — Apache-2.0
- xunit.abstractions, analyzers, assert, core, and extensibility packages — Apache-2.0
- Microsoft.NET.Test.Sdk — MIT
- Microsoft.CodeCoverage, Microsoft.TestPlatform.ObjectModel, and Microsoft.TestPlatform.TestHost — MIT
- coverlet.collector — MIT
- Newtonsoft.Json — MIT

Exact versions and the complete resolved graph are pinned in
`tests/DocRedock.Tests/packages.lock.json` and verified against
`licenses/allowlist.json`. CI emits `artifacts/sbom.cdx.json`.

Tesseract OCR is an optional external executable licensed under Apache-2.0.
The built-in PDF renderer bundles the variable Noto Sans JP font
(`src/DocRedock.Render/Assets/NotoSansJP[wght].ttf`) from Google Fonts, licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1). The upstream license text is
available at https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notosansjp/OFL.txt.
The bundled asset is sourced from the upstream `NotoSansJP[wght].ttf` at
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/notosansjp/NotoSansJP%5Bwght%5D.ttf
(SHA-256: c2f3b4d463500a2ddcd3849cded1fceeb9fd6d1c32e6cbecd568453ba50fc68f).
The font is embedded in generated PDF files so they remain portable.
